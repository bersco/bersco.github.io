I recently completed my PhD in Political Science at the Australian National University, specializing in political behaviour and metascience.

## Research Focus

My research examines democratic resilience and voter tolerance for undemocratic political behaviour. Using survey experiments and meta-analytic methods, I investigate why voters sometimes support illiberal politicians and how they might be persuaded to vote pro-democratically.

**Key Publications:**
- [When are Democracies at Risk of Democratic Decline?](https://psyarxiv.com/x4bw3/) - Meta-analysis and experimental study examining voter tolerance for undemocratic behaviour, with a focus on the Philippines
- [Data Availability and Research Transparency in Political Science](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rsos.240313) - Analysis of 20,000 publications in political science and international relations (Royal Society Open Science, 2024)
